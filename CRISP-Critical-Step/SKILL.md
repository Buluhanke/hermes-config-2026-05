# dl-research

CRISP — Critical Step Perception (arXiv:2608.01867): Training efficient deep search agents by distinguishing necessary evidence-gathering steps from redundant ones. Uses Backward Evidence Induction: strong model traverses completed trajectory backward, labels each step as critical/non-critical. Distills into smaller recognizer for single-pass classification. Efficiency-aware reward only on successful rollouts. Results: -15.1% interaction turns (BrowseComp), -33.2% (HLE-Verified), same accuracy.
