# RoTS-32B — 47.4% OSWorld SOTA via Error Recovery Trajectory Synthesis

**Paper**: arXiv 2605.29447 | **Published**: 28 May 2026 | **Venue**: ICML 2026 Spotlight

**Title**: Recovering Policy-Induced Errors: Benchmarking and Trajectory Synthesis for Robust GUI Agents

## Core Contributions

### 1. GUI-RobustEval — New Error Recovery Benchmark
- 1,216 executable test cases
- Systematically measures error recovery capabilities across realistic error modes
- Fills critical gap: existing benchmarks measure success rate but not recovery from failure

### 2. RoTS — Robustness-driven Trajectory Synthesis
- Scalable synthesis framework
- **Tree-based pipeline** proactively discovers diverse error modes
- Synthesizes corresponding recovery steps
- 800k high-quality training data

### 3. RoTS-32B Performance
- **OSWorld: 47.4%** success rate (State-of-the-Art)
- **All-Pass@4: 33.8%**
- Both RoTS-7B and RoTS-32B show gains on GUI-RobustEval and traditional benchmarks
- **Key finding**: Improved long-horizon error recovery → contributes to both robustness AND overall performance
