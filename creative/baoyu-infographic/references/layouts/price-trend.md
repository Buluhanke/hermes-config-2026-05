# price-trend

Time-series price visualization showing historical pricing, forecasts, and key inflection points.

## Structure

- Header: product/category name and date range
- Main chart area: line/area chart with price history
- Annotation layer: key events, price changes, promotions
- Summary stats: min, max, avg, current price
- Forecast zone (optional): projected prices with confidence band

## Best For

- Price history analysis
- Procurement budgeting
- Market timing decisions
- Supplier price negotiation
- Seasonal price pattern identification

## Visual Elements

- Line chart with smooth curves or stepped lines
- Area fill under line (semi-transparent)
- Data point markers at key dates
- Annotation callouts for events
- Shaded forecast region (dashed lines)
- Reference lines (MSRP, target price, last year)
- Mini sparklines for category breakdown

## Text Placement

- Title: "[Product/Category] Price Trend"
- Y-axis: Price (¥ CNY or USD)
- X-axis: Time (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Annotations: Event labels with arrows
- Stats box: current, min, max, avg, change %
- Legend at bottom or side

## Recommended Pairings

- `price-trend` + `corporate-memphis`: Business price reports
- `price-trend` + `technical-schematic`: Technical market analysis
- `price-trend` + `aged-academia`: Historical price research
