# supplier-comparison

Multi-supplier evaluation matrix with scoring across price, quality, lead time, and service dimensions.

## Structure

- Header: comparison title and criteria summary
- Supplier cards: each supplier as a vertical card with scores
- Radar/spider chart overlay or side-by-side bars
- Scoring legend at bottom
- Ranking indicator (crown/badge for top performer)

## Best For

- Supplier selection and evaluation
- Vendor comparison reports
- Procurement decision support
- Multi-vendor performance review

## Visual Elements

- Supplier logos/names as card headers
- Scoring bars or radar chart per supplier
- Color-coded scores (green=good, red=poor)
- Price, quality, lead time, service as criteria
- Overall score/ranking badge
- Star ratings or numeric scores in cells

## Text Placement

- Title at top
- Supplier names in first column or card headers
- Criteria labels on left axis
- Score values in cells/bars
- Legend below chart
- Source data notes at bottom

## Recommended Pairings

- `comparison-matrix` + `corporate-memphis`: Business supplier evaluations
- `comparison-matrix` + `morandi-journal`: Friendly vendor comparisons
- `comparison-matrix` + `technical-schematic`: Technical spec comparisons
