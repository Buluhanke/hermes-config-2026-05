# Supplier Cooperation Story Guide

Create narrative comics about supplier relationships, supply chain stories, and manufacturer-buyer partnerships.

## Content Type Profile

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Triggers | 供应商故事、产业链、供应链、厂商合作、贸易商故事 |
| Art Style | manga (default), ligne-claire |
| Tone | warm (default), dramatic |
| Layout | standard (default), cinematic |
| Aspect | 3:4 (portrait) |
| Page Count | 8-16 pages |

## Story Types

| Story Type | Description | Best For |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| Trust Building | Small buyer finds factory, grows together | 初创贸易商、电商卖家 |
| Quality Journey | Factory improves to meet buyer's standards | 品质提升故事、品控 |
| Crisis Response | Supplier handles emergency, wins loyalty | 供应商应急能力展示 |
| Expansion Partnership | Co-develop product, scale together | 柔性供应链故事 |
| Cultural Bridge | Cross-border supplier relationship | 跨境贸易、国际采购 |

## Story Arc Templates

### Arc 1: Trust Building (信任建立型)

```
Story Arc: Small buyer discovers factory → Initial order with caution → Quality issue handled well → Growing orders → Long-term partnership

5-Stage Structure:
Stage 1 (Opening): Buyer faces supplier reliability problem
Stage 2 (Inciting): Discovers new factory through referral
Stage 3 (Journey): Initial orders with careful verification
Stage 4 (Climax): Quality challenge emerges, factory responds well
Stage 5 (Resolution): Deep partnership, mutual growth
```

### Arc 2: Quality Journey (品质提升型)

```
Story Arc: Factory has quality gaps → New buyer requirements → Factory invests in improvement → Buyer witnesses transformation → Preferred supplier status

5-Stage Structure:
Stage 1 (Opening): Factory working at commodity level
Stage 2 (Challenge): Buyer presents higher standards
Stage 3 (Investment): Factory commits to upgrade (equipment, training)
Stage 4 (Proof): Inspection day, improvements validated
Stage 5 (Partnership): Preferred status, joint product development
```

### Arc 3: Crisis Partnership (危机共度型)

```
Story Arc: Buyer faces urgent order crisis → Supplier mobilizes beyond normal capacity → Crisis resolved → Supplier becomes strategic partner

5-Stage Structure:
Stage 1 (Opening): Normal stable relationship, comfortable volume
Stage 2 (Crisis): Buyer gets massive urgent order, normal lead time impossible
Stage 3 (Request): Buyer asks for impossible favor
Stage 4 (Response): Supplier mobilizes overtime, creative solutions
Stage 5 (Bond): Order delivered, relationship transformed to strategic
```

## Character Design for Supplier Stories

### Two-Character Structure (Most Common)

| Role | Archetype | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Buyer | 初创贸易商/电商/品牌方 | Young, eager, learning the industry |
| Supplier | 工厂老板/贸易商 | Experienced, values long-term relationships |
| Supporting | QC检验员/跟单员/老板娘 | Adds human texture to supplier side |

### Visual Differentiation

| Character Type | Visual Cues | Expression Range |
|----------------|-------------|------------------|
| Buyer (startup) | Casual business attire, laptop, always on phone | Eager → Stressed → Relieved → Grateful |
| Buyer (established) | Quality business wear, confident posture | Professional → Impressed → Satisfied → Trusting |
| Factory owner | Industrial aesthetic, safety vest sometimes, practical shoes | Welcoming → Confident → Responsive → Proud |
| Factory worker | Uniform, toolbelts, hands showing work | Focused → Skillful → Team player |

### Factory Setting Visualization

| Factory Type | Visual Elements |
|--------------|-----------------|
| 电子/机械 | Clean floors, organized stations, quality inspection equipment |
| 服装/纺织 | Fabric rolls, cutting tables, sewing machines, color coordination |
| 家具/家居 | Wood materials, finishing area, show room samples |
| 食品 | Stainless steel surfaces, hygiene protocols visible, packaging line |
| 玩具 | Bright colors, safety testing equipment, variety of product types |

## Supply Chain Story Elements

### Key Story Moments to Visualize

| Moment | Why Important | Visual Approach |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Initial factory visit | Trust foundation | Wide establishing shot, buyer taking notes, owner tour-guiding |
| Sample approval | First test passed | Sample room close-up, QC checklist visual, satisfied expression |
| First container | Relationship formalized | Container being loaded, handshake moment, invoice/contract visual |
| Quality inspection | Verification of standards | Inspector with clipboard, magnifying glass on detail, factory team watching |
| Problem resolution | Character building | Supplier working late, new solution demonstrated, buyer relief |
| Joint growth | Long-term value | Expanded factory, buyer's business growth shown in parallel panels |

### Supply Chain Visual Metaphors

| Concept | Visual Metaphor |
|---------|-----------------|
| Reliability | Solid foundation / anchored ship |
| Flexibility | Bamboo bending in wind / river routing around rocks |
| Quality consistency | Assembly line rhythm / puzzle pieces fitting perfectly |
| Partnership | Bridge between two sides / hands clasped together |
| Growth | Ascending staircase / seedlings growing in parallel |
| Trust | Shield protecting both parties / clear window between them |

## Storytelling Techniques for B2B Context

### Show, Don't Tell

| Instead of saying... | Show this visual... |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| "Good communication" | Late night chat with factory owner, time zone difference visible |
| "Quick response" | Same-day sample photos sent, timestamp in corner |
| "Quality products" | Side-by-side comparison, inspection stamp visible |
| "Flexible MOQ" | Small batch alongside regular container, visual size contrast |
| "Long-term partner" | Both companies' growth shown over multiple years |

### Emotional Beats for B2B Stories

B2B stories need human moments, not just business transactions:

| Scene | Emotional Beat |
|-------|---------------|
| Buyer's risk | Showing buyer's nervousness before first big order |
| Supplier's investment | Supplier buying new equipment for buyer's product |
| Shared achievement | Both celebrating successful product launch |
| Mutual understanding | Buyer visiting factory during holiday, cultural moment |
| Future vision | Joint planning session for next season's product |

## Quality Markers

- [ ] Story has clear arc with tension and resolution
- [ ] Both buyer and supplier characters are sympathetic
- [ ] Business context is authentic and accurate
- [ ] Visual elements ground the story in real supply chain context
- [ ] Platform elements (1688) add authenticity without overwhelming
- [ ] Final page shows mutual benefit / long-term relationship
- [ ] Story could be used for supplier marketing or buyer education
- [ ] Chinese text uses full-width punctuation
